I build the whole system.

Not one layer — the complete stack, across every platform type.

Over 5+ years I've built production systems at every layer — perception, SLAM, state estimation, control, navigation, and learning — and deployed them on real hardware across different robot types.

On the perception and mapping side: I've implemented 2D grid SLAM (GMapping, Hector), 3D LiDAR-based mapping and localization (NDT/ICP, Point-LIO, RTAB-Map), and vision-based SLAM (ORB-SLAM2, EKF-VIO, Visual Graph SLAM ). My MSc thesis at Bristol evaluated ORB-SLAM2 vs DeepVO on the full KITTI benchmark, integrating DeepLab v3+ semantic masking to assess trajectory robustness under dynamic scene conditions.

On the control and navigation side: I've written RTOS algorithms for a 6-DOF surgical arm achieving high-precision control, stabilize drones in GPS-denied environments, implemented 3D Navigation Stack for aerial collision avoidance with MPC-based recovery, and fine-tuned Nav2 on the Scout Mini AMR to 5cm positioning accuracy. I've also applied PPO and SAC reinforcement learning to 7-DOF robotic manipulation in Gazebo Harmonic.

The platforms I've deployed on: surgical manipulators, quadcopters, fixed-wing UAVs, autonomous ground vehicles, and AMRs. Different kinematics, different failure modes, different real-time constraints — same engineering rigour applied throughout.
